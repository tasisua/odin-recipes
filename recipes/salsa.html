Salsa Recipe

Home

Description

Homemade salsa with ripe tomatoes, onion, jalapenos and a few other fresh ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomatillo, diced (optional)
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • salt to taste
  • 1 jalapeno, minced

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine tomatoes, onion, tomatillo, cilantro, garlic, lime juice, and salt in a medium-sized mixing bowl. Mix well
  3. Add 1/2 of the jalapeno pepper and taste. If you desire your salsa with more kick, add remaining 1/2 jalapeno.
  4. Cover the salsa and chill until ready to serve.
