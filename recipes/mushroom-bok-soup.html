Mushroom Bok Choy Soup Recipe

Home

Description

This recipe includes mushrooms, bok choy, ginger and garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 pound white and cremini mushrooms, cut into quarters
  • 1 head bok choy, chopped plus extra green leaves for garnish
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic, or more to taste
  • 8 slices fresh ginger, quartered
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 7 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Heat butter in a stockpot over medium heat. Add mushrooms, bok choy, green onions, garlic, and ginger. Sprinkle mushroom mixture with lime juice, then cook and stir just until slightly browned, 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Ladle into bowls and garnish with cilantro and green bok choy leaves.
